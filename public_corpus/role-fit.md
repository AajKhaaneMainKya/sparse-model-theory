# Role Fit

The public corpus supports Rahul as a fit for roles that value AI product judgment, founder/operator context, ambiguous problem framing, and practical implementation across backend, retrieval, prompting, and UI.

The strongest fit signal is not a single framework. It is the repeated pattern of turning open-ended reasoning tasks into bounded product surfaces: explicit schemas, retrieval limits, caveats, model fallback behavior, and interfaces that show what is known versus unknown.

For founder/operator or AI product roles, interviewers should probe how Rahul scopes ambiguous ideas, decides when a prototype is enough, handles missing evidence, and chooses between deterministic logic, retrieval, and model calls.
